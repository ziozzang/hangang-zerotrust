## Why this round happened

The owner judged the admin consoles unacceptable ("this cannot be an admin"),
Kubernetes support ambiguous and Hopae incomplete (e.g. passkeys), and set the bar
to **commercial-product quality** (Cloudflare Zero Trust / Okta / Teleport level).
Design was recorded as ADRs 0019–0022 (merged to this repository's `main`), then
implemented across the component repositories as reviewable PRs.

  Prometheus metrics, operator passkey requirement with Ed25519 operator assertions,

  Kubernetes workload identity (ns/SA, offline JWKS, SPIFFE), PostgreSQL HA store.

  Hopae projection, templates, deny, metadata, shadow/cutover), SA principals,
